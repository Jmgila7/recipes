Ingredients you will need

For the dish

  • 2 thick slices of bread
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 2 slices of Flamengo cheese
  • 2 slices of turkey or chicken ham
  • 2 slices of bacon (optional)
  • 1 poultry sausage (optional)

For the sauce

  • 1 small onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 200 ml tomato purée
  • 100 ml beer
  • 100 ml chicken stock
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Piri-piri sauce, to taste
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Preparation

  1. Finely chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a saucepan and sauté the onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add the tomato purée, beer, chicken stock, bay leaf, piri-piri sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Let the sauce simmer for 15–20 minutes, then remove the bay leaf and blend until smooth.
  5. Season the chicken breast with salt and black pepper, then cook it in a frying pan until fully cooked.
  6. Cook the bacon and poultry sausage, if using.
  7. Place one slice of bread on a plate and add the chicken, ham, bacon, and sausage.
  8. Cover with the second slice of bread and place the cheese slices on top.
  9. Place the sandwich in the oven until the cheese melts.
  10. Pour the hot sauce over the sandwich.
  11. Serve immediately with french fries, if desired.
