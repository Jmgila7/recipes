Ingredients you will need

  • 500 g tipo 00 flour
  • 325 ml water
  • 2 g fresh yeast (or 1 g dry yeast)
  • 15 g salt
  • 200 g San Marzano tomatoes, crushed
  • 250 g fresh mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • A pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Mix the flour and water in a large bowl until combined.
  2. Add the yeast and salt, then knead the dough for about 10–15 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rise until doubled in size, approximately 2–4 hours at room temperature.
  4. Divide the dough into 3 or 4 equal portions and shape them into balls.
  5. Cover the dough balls and let them rest for another 2–4 hours.
  6. Preheat the oven to its highest temperature, ideally 250°C or higher. If you have a pizza stone or steel, preheat it as well.
  7. Crush the San Marzano tomatoes and season them with a pinch of salt.
  8. Stretch each dough ball gently with your hands, leaving a thicker edge around the crust.
  9. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce over the dough.
  10. Add pieces of fresh mozzarella, a few basil leaves, and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.
  11. Bake the pizza until the crust is puffed and golden and the cheese has melted. The time will depend on your oven temperature.
  12. Remove the pizza from the oven, let it cool briefly, and serve immediately.
